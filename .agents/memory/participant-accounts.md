name: Participant account requirements
description: Participants use their own TREASURE account, not a Replit login.
---

Peserta membuat akun situs dengan username, email, dan kata sandi. Pendaftaran peserta tidak boleh mewajibkan akun atau login Replit. Email tetap diminta.

**Why:** The user explicitly selected “Membuat akun situs dengan username, email, dan kata sandi” after asking for registration without Replit login.

**How to apply:** Keep participant signup/sign-in native to the registration journey. Do not restore participant Replit login buttons. Changes to participant login do not authorize changes to organizer access.
