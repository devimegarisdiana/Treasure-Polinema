---
name: Organizer access and notification recipients
description: Distinguishes authorized organizers from notification recipients.
---

Keep organizer access separate from the email notification recipient. Receiving notifications does not automatically grant dashboard or document access.

**Why:** The user deliberately supplied different accounts for organizer login and registration notifications. The dashboard contains private student IDs and payment documents.

**How to apply:** Obtain explicit authorization before changing which accounts can access organizer data. Do not infer admin rights from a participant email, an email recipient, or a successful participant sign-in.
